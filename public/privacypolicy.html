The Blue Fit - Privacy Policy

Health data we read

If you allow it in Health Connect, The Blue Fit reads your steps, sleep, active calories and heart rate for the current day. We only read these values; we never write to or change your Health Connect data.

Why we read it

The numbers are shown to you on your Today screen and to your assigned coach, so your coach can follow your progress and adjust your plan.

Where it goes

Daily totals are sent over an encrypted connection to The Blue Fit servers and stored against your account. Only you, your coach and the administrators of your coaching programme can see them. We do not sell your health data and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can stop sharing at any time: open Health Connect, choose App permissions, select The Blue Fit and remove the permissions. You can also ask your coach or administrator to delete your account and its data.
