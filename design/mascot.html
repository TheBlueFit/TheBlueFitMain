Your reference sheets showed the same character in three finishes. I picked one finish and made three app-ready poses on the app's own navy so they drop straight into the UI.

The three finishes from your sheets

Not used

Cinematic render

Warm gym lighting, soft shading, detailed anatomy. Gorgeous as a poster, but the internal detail turns to mush below ~120px and it fights the app's flat dark surfaces.

Not used

Glossy illustration

Clean but still airbrushed — gradients and highlights make him look like a different character next to the app's gold monogram and outlined icons.

Picked

Flat vector / bold outline

Thick black keyline, two-tone cel shading, flat fills. Reads instantly from 24px to full width, holds up on every one of the five themes, and matches the app's existing line-icon language.
